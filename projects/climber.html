FIRST Robotics · 2025

Competition Robot Climber

A major robot subassembly that I worked on from early prototypes through the first competition-ready design, then continued redesigning after competition exposed problems that were difficult to find beforehand.

My Role Mechanical design, prototyping, machining, assembly, testing, redesign
Primary CAD Autodesk Inventor
Environment FIRST Robotics Competition

01 · Development

Prototype to first competition

I prototyped and designed the climber toward the first version that was ready for competition. My work included CAD, machining, assembly, and hands-on development of the mechanism.

The goal was not only to create a design in CAD, but to get a real mechanism built and working on the competition robot.

02 · Testing

Competition found the problems

After the team's Week 1 competition, we had real operating experience with the climber and found both known and newly discovered issues. That gave me a much better picture of what needed to change than shop testing alone could provide.

The important part of this project was the iteration: the first competition-ready design was not treated as the end of the design process.

03 · Redesign

Iterating through competition season

During competition season I continued redesigning the climber to address the issues we found. We tested the redesigned mechanism and found it to be more reliable, easier to use, and lighter.

That process carried the climber from the early prototype stage through multiple real-world revisions into the final configuration used later in the season.

04 · What this project shows

Design is an iterative process

  • Turning prototypes into manufacturable mechanical hardware
  • Using CAD as part of a build-test-redesign loop
  • Machining and assembling the parts I was designing
  • Learning from failures and problems found in actual competition
  • Improving a mechanism instead of treating the first working version as finished
